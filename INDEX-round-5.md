# Reference images, round five

> **Note added when this set was placed (2026-11-19).** The delivery had its pictures in four folders
> (`1-trunk`, `2-shoulder-arm`, `3-hands`, `4-feet`) and an `ADULTS-ONLY/` folder beside them; here they
> are in this repository's numbered folders, which go on from the earlier ones (`15-trunk/`,
> `16-shoulder-arm/`, `17-hands/`, `18-feet/`, and the same three under `ADULTS-ONLY/`), and the paths
> below say where. **Every one of the 217 pictures was looked at by whoever placed it before it went
> in.** What was changed from the delivery:
>
> - Two dissections of the trunk from Sobotta's atlas (figures 245 and 250) show the groin of the body
>   dissected. They were delivered among the clothed and skinless plates and are placed under
>   `ADULTS-ONLY/15-trunk/` with the unclothed figures.
> - Two line drawings from Munson (figures 7 and 41) were saved with a transparent ground, black lines
>   on nothing, which shows as a black rectangle in most viewers. They are flattened onto white.
>   Nothing else was altered.
> - No picture was left out. None shows a child. The 31 unclothed figures of the delivery are all
>   Paul Richer's drawings of his adult male model (a muscular man with a moustache, drawn from life
>   for *Nouvelle anatomie artistique*) or the outline keys to those drawings. No plate of Muybridge's
>   is in this set, so there was nothing to hold against his catalogue.
> - The photographs of hands and feet show hands and feet only, with no faces.

This set answers Specula's `docs/research/REFERENCE_ROUND5_BRIEF.md`: the trunk as a surface, the
shoulder and the arm where it joins, hands, and feet. It was gathered and indexed on 2026-11-19, and
a separate checker then re-read the licence and the author of all 217 from each Wikimedia Commons file
page (212 as stated; 5 Wellcome files have no artist field, and their author is taken from the
Wellcome description on the same page). The numbers that came with it (hand and foot dimensions, joint
ranges, joint centres, trunk sections, muscle thickness, skinfolds) are in Specula's
`docs/research/library/`, files A9 to A16.

**Licences:** 180 public domain, 11 CC0, 12 CC BY-SA 4.0, 7 CC BY-SA 2.1 JP (Anatomography's renders
of BodyParts3D), 5 CC BY 4.0, 2 CC BY 2.0. A CC BY or CC BY-SA picture is credited as its row says;
a CC BY-SA picture that is changed is shared under the same licence.

**Sizes:** where a file is smaller than its original, it is one of Commons' own smaller renders
(scaled, never traced); the last column but one gives the saved size, and the original's in brackets
where they differ. Files under 2,000 pixels are at their source's largest: the four plates of Braune,
several figures of Sobotta, two of Munson, and the text figures of Richer's first volume.

**A wrong tag on Commons, noted by the checker:** Braune's plates carry the tag "Original work of the
US Federal Government". That is wrong for a German atlas of 1867 to 1872; the work is in the public
domain by its age all the same.

**Almost every picture here shows a man**: Richer's model, and the male bodies of Sobotta's,
Spalteholz's and Braune's atlases. A woman's trunk as a surface, at this level of detail and under an
open licence, was not found.
