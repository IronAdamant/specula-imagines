# Adults only (18+)

The 52 pictures in this folder show unclothed adult human bodies, genitals included: living figures
drawn from the model, figures shown as muscle without skin, and photographs of men and women walking,
running, jumping and climbing (a few of the men wear a loincloth). They are academic anatomy, figure
and motion studies from the 16th to the 19th century (Dürer, Albinus, Paul Richer, C. H. Stratz,
John Vanderpoel, Eadweard Muybridge, and Caudron's sculpted muscle figure), kept because a body's
proportions, its surface and its gait cannot be studied on a clothed one. Nothing here is sexual, and
no picture of a child is here: those were left out when the sets were gathered, and one drawing whose
subjects could not be shown to be adults was taken out.

They are kept apart from the rest so that nobody meets them without being told first.
