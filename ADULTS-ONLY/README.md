# Adults only (18+)

The 24 pictures in this folder show unclothed human bodies, genitals included: living figures drawn
from the model, figures shown as muscle without skin, and photographs of men and women walking and
running. They are academic anatomy and motion studies from the 18th and 19th centuries (Albinus,
Paul Richer, Eadweard Muybridge, and Caudron's sculpted muscle figure), kept because a body's
proportions, its surface and its gait cannot be studied on a clothed one. Nothing here is sexual.

They are kept apart from the rest so that nobody meets them without being told first.
