# Reference images, round four

> **Nine plates taken out on 2026-11-19.** Muybridge's own *Prospectus and Catalogue of Plates* (1887)
> gives a model's number for every plate and says who the models were. His men who walk, run and jump
> are "students or graduates of The University of Pennsylvania, young men aged from eighteen to
> twenty-four"; the only boys are models 27, 28 and 29 ("aged thirteen to fifteen"), and none of their
> plates was ever here. Of the women he says that models 4 to 13, 15 and 19 are "unmarried, of ages
> varying from seventeen to twenty-four", and of models 2 and 20 he gives no age. A woman who may have
> been seventeen cannot be shown to be an adult, so every unclothed plate of those models is out of this
> repository: plates 19 (model 20), 71 (model 7), 73, 76, 155 and 182 (model 8), 92 and 129 (model 2),
> and the Wellcome photogravure V0048621 (plate 16 by its rows of ten: model 4). Their rows are taken
> out of the tables below. The women's plates that stay are of model 1, "a widow, aged thirty-five".
> The catalogue: https://archive.org/details/cu31924024580353 (pages 12 and 13 for the models).

> **Note added when this set was placed (2026-11-19).** The delivery had its pictures in one folder and
> an `ADULTS-ONLY/` folder beside it; here they are in this repository's numbered folders, and the
> paths below say where (`14-clothing-patterns/` is new). Every picture was looked at again by whoever
> placed it. **Two plates of the delivery are not here** (10 of its 12 are): Muybridge's plates 8 and 9,
> a slender young man walking. Their source titles him a man and nothing suggests otherwise, but his
> age cannot be shown from the plate, and this repository keeps no unclothed figure whose subject
> cannot be shown to be an adult. They wait for the owner's word; their pages are
> https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Man_in_pelvis_cloth_walking_(rbm-QP301M8-1887-008).jpg and
> https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Man_in_pelvis_cloth_walking_(rbm-QP301M8-1887-009).jpg.
> A second checker confirmed all twelve licences as stated (2026-11-18).

Gap 6 (walk-cycle key-pose sheets; matched front/side/back body sets) and gap 8 (open flat patterns). Every image was opened and checked by eye on 2026-11-17 (Australia/Sydney). Licences were read from each Wikimedia Commons file page (extmetadata LicenseShortName). Downloads are no more than 1,920 px on the long side; the small originals were kept at full size. Transparent SVG renders were flattened onto white by me.

Rules: clothed or plainly adult subjects only. Unclothed or near-unclothed adults are in ADULTS-ONLY/. No children. Rejected after viewing: "A man walking. Photogravure after Eadweard Muybridge, 1887. Wellcome V0048750": unclothed, and the subject could not be confirmed as an adult, so it is not included or linked. Also rejected: Descriptive Zoopraxography 046 (a horse, off-topic) and "Walk and run cycle.jpg" (download blocked by HTTP 429).
